# Face cream jar filling

We fill 50 ml face cream into round white plastic jars (65 mm across, 45 mm tall) and close them with a lid.

About 1200 jars per 8-hour shift.

Steps: the empty jar is filled with cream, the lid is pressed on, a label goes on the side, and jars are checked by a camera (every bad jar is pushed off). Finished jars are packed six to a case.

Clean room, little floor space: keep the line short.
