# Desktop 3D printer assembly

We assemble open-frame desktop 3D printers on carriers (pallets) that ride a pallet conveyor and return underneath.

Target: one printer every 90 seconds (40 per hour).

Stations, in order:
1. Kitting: parts are picked from totes onto the carrier.
2. Frame: the frame is built and screwed together by a gantry robot.
3. Wiring: the harness is laid and plugged in.
4. Calibration and test: the printer is powered and tested.
5. Inspection: a scanner checks it; failed printers go to a reject lane.
6. Packaging: good printers are packed into a carton with foam and dispatched.

Operators work at stations 1 and 3. Guarded cells with fences.
