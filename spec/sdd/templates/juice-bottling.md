# Apple juice bottling

We bottle cloudy apple juice in 1 litre glass bottles (about 90 mm across, 260 mm tall) with a red screw cap and a paper label.

Target: 240 bottles per hour.

What happens to each bottle:
1. Fill it with juice through a nozzle (about 6 seconds of filling).
2. Screw on the cap.
3. Put the label on.
4. A camera checks the fill level, the cap and the label; bad bottles go off the line.

Food grade, stainless steel frames. One bottle at a time on a belt.
