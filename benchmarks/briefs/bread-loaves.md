# Sliced bread packing

Baked sandwich loaves (about 280 × 110 × 120 mm) come out of the oven and are packed.

About 1200 loaves per hour.

The loaves cool on the way, are sliced, put into a plastic bag, the bag is closed with a clip and a date label goes on. A check weigher rejects light loaves. Packed loaves go into crates.

Food grade, high hygiene.
