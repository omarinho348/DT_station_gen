# Tablet blister packing

We pack tablets into blister strips of 10 (strip 95 × 45 mm).

Target: 60 strips per minute.

The blister cavities are formed from PVC film by heating, tablets are dropped into the cavities, a camera checks every cavity is filled, aluminium lid foil is heat-sealed on, the strips are cut apart, and two strips go into each folding carton with a leaflet.

GMP clean room. Strips with an empty cavity are rejected.
