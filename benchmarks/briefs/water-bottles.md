# Still water bottling

We bottle still mineral water in 0.5 L PET bottles (65 mm across, 210 mm tall) with a blue screw cap and a wrap-around label.

Target: 720 bottles per hour.

1. Fill each bottle (about 3 seconds of filling).
2. Screw on the cap.
3. Put on the label.
4. A camera checks fill level and cap; about one bottle in twenty fails and is pushed off the line.
5. Good bottles leave the line for shrink-packing into six-packs (done elsewhere).

Food grade. One bottle at a time on a belt.
