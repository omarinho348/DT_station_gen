# Control board assembly

We assemble small control boards (PCBs, 160 × 100 mm, 1.6 mm thick) on carriers that ride a pallet conveyor and return underneath.

Target: one board every 45 seconds.

1. Solder paste is dispensed onto the pads.
2. A SCARA robot places about 20 components from feeders.
3. The board goes through a reflow oven to solder the parts.
4. Automated optical inspection checks every board; failed boards go to a reject lane.
5. Good boards are taken off the carrier into a tote.

ESD-safe area, guarded cells.
