# Furniture panel cutting

We cut chipboard furniture panels (raw boards up to 2.0 × 0.8 m, 18 mm thick) to size and prepare them for assembly.

About 90 panels per hour.

Each panel is sawn to size, the visible edges get an edge band glued on, hinge and dowel holes are drilled, and finished panels are stacked on a pallet. An operator checks dimensions now and then.

Dust extraction at the saw and drill.
