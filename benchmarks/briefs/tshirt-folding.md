# T-shirt folding and packing

We fold and pack printed cotton t-shirts for online orders.

About 400 shirts per hour.

A shirt is laid flat by an operator, folded by the machine to 250 × 300 mm, slipped into a poly bag, the bag is sealed and an address label is printed and stuck on. Bags go into a tote for the shipping area.

Two operators: loading and quality.
