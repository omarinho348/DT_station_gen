# Battery module assembly

We build battery modules: 12 cylindrical cells in a plastic module frame (300 × 120 × 70 mm), on carriers on a pallet conveyor.

Target: 20 modules per hour.

Stations:
1. Cell loading: a gantry picks cells from a tray and places them into the frame.
2. Busbar placement: the copper busbars are laid on the cell tops.
3. Laser welding: each busbar is welded to the cell terminals.
4. End-of-line test: voltage and insulation are tested; failed modules go to a reject lane.

Guarded cells, no people inside.
