# Car door inner module

We pre-assemble car door inner modules (the trim carrier, about 1.0 × 0.8 m) on fixtures that ride a pallet conveyor.

Target: one module every 90 seconds.

1. The window regulator is placed on the carrier and fastened with 4 screws.
2. The speaker is fitted and fastened with 3 screws.
3. The wiring harness is laid and plugged into the regulator and speaker.
4. Function test: the window motor and speaker are switched on and checked.

One operator per station, screwdrivers on balancers; the rest automatic.
