# Body lotion filling

We fill 250 ml body lotion into white oval plastic bottles (60 × 40 mm, 170 mm tall) and close them with a pump head.

About 2400 bottles per 8-hour shift.

Each bottle is filled with lotion (thick, about 5 seconds), the pump head is placed on and screwed down, a front label goes on, and the bottle is weighed and checked by a camera. Rejects are taken off the line. Finished bottles are packed twelve to a case.

Clean room, operators only at the end of the line.
