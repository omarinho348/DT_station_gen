# Coffee capsule filling

We fill aluminium coffee capsules (37 mm across, 28 mm tall) with ground coffee.

Target: 3600 capsules per hour, filled in groups of 6.

Capsules are filled with coffee by weight, the coffee is tamped, a foil lid is sealed on, a camera checks every lid, and the capsules are packed ten to a box. Capsules with a bad seal are rejected.

Food grade.
